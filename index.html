RAVEN is a curated thrift catalog born in Algiers. Every piece is hand-selected, photographed, and listed once. No duplicates. No mass stock.

We believe pre-owned clothing should feel intentional — and affordable. Good prices for everyone, because second-hand is for everyone.

Browse the drop, find your piece, and message us directly on WhatsApp to reserve it. Every garment you buy breathes new life instead of becoming waste.
